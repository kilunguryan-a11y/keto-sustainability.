01 / WHO WE ARE

A broader view of the environment

Environmental challenges don't happen in isolation. Neither should their solutions.

Keton Sustainability is an emerging applied research initiative built around a simple conviction: better solutions begin with better questions. We look beyond symptoms to investigate the scientific, technological, economic and institutional forces shaping environmental outcomes.

Our scope is intentionally broad — from ecosystems and water to energy systems, waste, climate, cities and the decisions that connect them. The goal is to turn credible evidence into useful action.

03 HOW WE WORK

From a difficult question to a workable solution.

Good research is more than gathering information. It is a disciplined process for moving from uncertainty toward decisions that can stand up to scrutiny.

THE KETON
RESEARCH CYCLE

05 WHAT GUIDES US

Useful research earns its place in the world.

01

Evidence before certainty. Be clear about what is known, what is assumed and what still needs investigation.

02

Systems over silos. Understand how ecological, technical, economic and institutional factors interact.

03

Impact over appearance. Judge ideas by their usefulness, feasibility and real-world outcomes — not how impressive they sound.

04

Learn in the open. Value collaboration, transparent methods and honest communication of limitations.
